# Voice & Tone

Derived from the actual language used across the corpus (not a separate style guide — SMM doesn't have one in the source material; this is pattern-extracted).

## Character
Direct, specific, professional, human, confident — **no inflated claims**. Explicit AI-knowledge-base rule: *"Preserve brand voice: direct, specific, professional, human, confident, no inflated claims."*

Systems-focused rather than task-focused; premium and operational rather than "generic creative agency."

## Signature lines (use verbatim, don't paraphrase)
- *"We don't just market your business. We build the system that grows it."*
- *"Stop Losing Leads You Already Paid For."*
- *"Never Miss the Opportunity."*
- *"Find the leak. Fix the system. Grow with control."*

## Patterns observed in the source writing
- **Diagnose before pitching.** Sales copy and scripts consistently lead with the prospect's operational gap, not a service list: *"The sales conversation diagnoses the business problem better than the prospect can describe it."*
- **Label estimates as estimates.** Recurring instruction across multiple docs: modeled/illustrative revenue-at-risk numbers must be shown as estimates, never as guaranteed recovered revenue or attributed revenue without data support.
- **Never invent.** Repeated guardrail across the AI knowledge rules and automation playbook: never invent client results, testimonials, prices, partnerships, integrations, or guarantees; say "not in my sources" / escalate rather than guess.
- **Numbers over adjectives.** Discovery questions and reporting templates favor concrete metrics (response time, attempt counts, close rate) over vague claims.

## What to avoid
Fear-based or overly dark presentation, clutter, generic stock-agency styling, guaranteed-outcome language, unsupported attribution claims.
