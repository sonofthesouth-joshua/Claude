# Company Profile

**Shadow Marketing Media (SMM)** is an AI-powered revenue-systems and growth agency led by founder/CEO **Joshua Carney**. It operates in the United States.

SMM's commercial focus is not selling isolated marketing tasks — it identifies where revenue is leaking *after* a business already generates demand (leads, calls, form fills), then builds the systems to capture, respond to, convert, retain, and expand that customer value.

## Positioning
- Core positioning: *"We don't just market your business. We build the system that grows it."*
- Primary market message: *"Stop Losing Leads You Already Paid For."*
- Brand promise: *"Never Miss the Opportunity."*
- Operating philosophy: *Find the leak. Fix the system. Grow with control.*

## The Shadow Growth System™
SMM's core operating framework, in five stages:

1. **Attract** — create qualified demand (SEO, AEO/GEO, paid media, social/content, brand, websites)
2. **Capture** — turn attention into identifiable opportunities (landing pages, forms, calls, chat, Revenue Leak Audit, lead tracking)
3. **Convert** — respond quickly and move leads toward revenue (AI receptionist, CRM, SMS/email, booking, sales follow-up, pipeline)
4. **Retain** — protect customer value after the sale (reviews, reputation, reactivation, communication, reporting)
5. **Expand** — increase account value and market share (upsells, referrals, multi-location systems, strategic growth)

## Ideal client profile
Businesses that already have revenue, demand, leads, or marketing spend, but are losing opportunity because response, follow-up, sales process, CRM use, estimates, reviews, or retention are weak.

**Priority verticals:** roofing/restoration/HVAC/plumbing/contractors and home services; med spas, clinics, dental, chiropractic, wellness; law firms and professional services; insurance; nonprofits; outdoor brands/outfitters/guides; multi-location and established service companies.

**Economic fit:** established businesses roughly $500K–$20M+ in annual revenue, already spending on demand generation and needing stronger conversion infrastructure.

## Brand
- Personality: confident, direct, human, relationship-first, ROI-grounded; systems-focused rather than task-focused; premium and operational without looking like a generic creative agency; technology-forward while keeping human sales conversations and storytelling central.
- Visual: black, white, and orange. Orange = CTA/emphasis/data highlights. Black = authority/typography/premium framing. Light neutrals = readability/trust surfaces. Avoid overly dark, fear-based, cluttered, or generic-stock-agency styling.
- Origin story: the name is tied to Joshua Carney's dog Shadow — loyalty, trust, dedication, support. Meant to inform client experience, not become sentimental marketing copy.
