## 1. Executive Company Profile

The company connects marketing, lead capture, AI response, CRM automation, sales follow-up, reputation, reporting, and retention into one operating system. ## 6. Signature Lead Magnet: Shadow Revenue Leak Audit

The Revenue Leak Audit is the primary diagnostic offer. It is designed to show businesses how much opportunity may be at risk from operational gaps after lead generation. Roofing has been the initial specialized version.

### Inputs

- Monthly marketing spend
- Monthly lead volume
- Percentage of leads beginning by phone
- Missed-call percentage
- Average closed-job value
- Gross margin percentage
- Current lead-to-job close rate
- First-response-time band
- Percentage of leads receiving three or more follow-up attempts
- Percentage of open estimates receiving follow-up
- Open estimates per month
- Google rating

### Leak Categories

- Missed calls
- Slow lead response
- Insufficient follow-up
- Stale or unworked estimates
- Weak review capture and response
- Poor pipeline ownership and visibility

### Audit Conversion Objective

1. Collect business and contact data.
2. Calculate an illustrative revenue-at-risk estimate.
3. Explain which operating leaks are most material.
4. Assign lead score / qualification data.
5. Route the lead into CRM.
6. Prompt the prospect to schedule a 30-minute strategy call.
7. Give Joshua a call-prep brief with questions tailored to that audit.
Audit outputs must be framed as estimates and diagnostic modeling, not guaranteed recovered revenue.

## 7. Sales Process


### Sales Principle

The sales conversation diagnoses the business problem better than the prospect can describe it. SMM should not lead with a list of marketing services. It should uncover what happens after the lead arrives and quantify the operational cost of weak response and follow-up.

### Discovery Flow

1. Establish current revenue, team size, locations, average job/client value, and growth target.
2. Identify monthly marketing spend, lead sources, lead volume, and current close rate.
3. Map what happens from first call/form/chat through booked appointment, quote, close, fulfillment, review, and referral.
4. Inspect speed-to-lead, missed calls, follow-up attempts, stale estimates/opportunities, no-shows, CRM usage, and ownership.
5. Quantify the gap using conservative assumptions and clearly label modeled values.
6. Show the future-state system using the Shadow Growth System.
7. Recommend scope based on the actual leak, not a preset bundle.
8. Confirm decision process, timeline, implementation requirements, and budget.
9. Present proposal, contract, payment, and 3–7 day deployment expectation.
10. Move closed client directly into onboarding.

### Core Discovery Questions

- Where do your leads go the moment they come in?
- Who owns the first response and what is the actual response time?
- What happens to missed calls?
- How many follow-up attempts happen before a lead is considered lost?
- Who follows open estimates or proposals, and for how long?
- Can you see lead source, appointment, opportunity, closed revenue, and lost reason in one place?
- What percentage of customers are asked for a review?
- What happens to old leads and past customers?
- If lead volume doubled next month, would your current process convert it or expose more leaks?

## 9. Delivery and Quality Control


### Minimum Launch QA

- Every form creates a contact and opportunity where appropriate.
- Every call source is tracked or intentionally excluded.
- Missed calls trigger the correct recovery workflow.

- AI responses use approved business knowledge and escalation rules.
