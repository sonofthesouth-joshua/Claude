# Offer & Pricing

**The company has used more than one pricing ladder while refining the model** (source's own words). The current core sales range is **$5,000–$15,000/month**, with lower-entry and enterprise tiers as controlled exceptions.

## Pricing ladder

| Offer | Price | Role in ladder | Typical scope | Term |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation / Entry | $2,500–$5,000/mo | Controlled entry | Core website/CRM/reputation/automation components; narrower scope | Custom |
| Growth | $5,000/mo | Core | Revenue-system foundation, lead capture, follow-up, reporting, selected demand channels | 3/6/12 months |
| Scale | $7,500–$10,000/mo | Core premium | Deeper automation, AI response, CRM, conversion, SEO/ads, reputation | 3/6/12 months |
| Authority / Dominance | $10,000–$15,000/mo | Flagship | Integrated acquisition + conversion + retention system with strategic oversight | 6/12 months |
| Enterprise | $15,000–$30,000+/mo | Custom | Multi-location, complex integrations, higher lead volume, advanced reporting/strategy | Custom |

## Service families (what's sold within the offers)
Revenue Infrastructure (CRM design, pipeline, routing, dashboards) · AI Response (AI receptionist, chat, qualification, booking, escalation) · Follow-Up Automation (missed-call text-back, SMS/email nurture, reactivation) · Web & Conversion (sites, funnels, CRO, tracking) · Search & Authority (SEO/AEO/GEO, reputation) · Paid Demand (Google/Meta ads) · Content & Social · Reputation · Analytics & Strategy · Partnership/Resale (Vendasta-backed, white-label, software/AI resale).

## Contract structure
- Terms offered: 3, 6, or 12 months.
- Deployment target: system live 3–7 days after signed agreement and confirmed payment, regardless of term.
- Longer terms explicitly favored for build/testing/optimization/seasonal learning and compounding results (6-mo) and iterative growth/retention/channel optimization (12-mo, "best operating horizon").

## Lower-ticket / non-retainer products
For prospects not ready for a full managed engagement: audits, consulting, coaching, templates, implementation sprints, DFY funnels, white-label systems, software/AI setup, educational products. **No prices defined** — source explicitly says these "should be stored as configurable fields until Joshua approves the final catalog."
