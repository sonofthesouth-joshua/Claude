## Commission Framework

| Component | Recommended Starting Rule | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SDR booked meeting | Flat bounty only after qualified meeting shows | Avoid paying for low-quality calendar fills |
| Closer new MRR | 8-12% of first-month collected revenue | Pay on collected cash, not signed paper |
| Closer prepaid contract | 3-6% of collected contract cash | Margin-check before approval |
| Expansion | 5-10% of first-month expansion collected | Credit documented owner |
| Retention bonus | Quarterly bonus tied to gross retention + client health | Do not reward retention at negative margin |
| Clawback | Apply if refund/nonpayment inside defined window | Final legal/comp policy required |
