SHADOW MARKETING MEDIA
PROPOSAL & CLIENT REPORTING TEMPLATES
Reusable structure for sales and account management

## Proposal Structure

- Client situation and diagnosed revenue problem
- Current-state evidence and assumptions
- Recommended Shadow Growth System scope
- Deliverables and exclusions
- Implementation plan and 3–7 day target
- KPIs and reporting
- Client responsibilities
- Investment and term
- Change-control / third-party costs
- Acceptance

## 3-Month Positioning

Fast implementation and focused proof period. Best when scope is narrow and the client can supply access/approvals quickly.

## 6-Month Positioning

Adds enough operating time for testing, optimization, seasonal learning, pipeline recovery, and stronger adoption.

## 12-Month Positioning

Supports iterative acquisition, conversion, retention, reputation, and channel optimization across a longer operating cycle.

## Monthly Client Report

- Executive summary: what changed and why it matters
- Demand metrics
- Response metrics
- Sales metrics
- Revenue/attribution where supported
- Follow-up and stale pipeline
- Reputation
- Risks and dependencies
- Top three next actions with owners and dates

## Reporting Guardrail

Do not claim revenue attribution unless the data supports it. Distinguish observed metrics from modeled estimates.
