SHADOW MARKETING MEDIA
ROLE SCORECARDS & COMP FRAMEWORK
Accountability model for scaling SMM

## CEO

Joshua owns vision, offer architecture, strategic sales, partnerships, brand, major accounts, and capital allocation.

## SDR

- Outcome: qualified meetings that show.
- Track: prospecting volume, positive replies, booked meetings, qualification, show rate.
- Comp starting concept: bounty only for qualified meetings that show.

## Closer

- Outcome: profitable collected new revenue.
- Track: discovery-to-proposal, proposal-to-close, average contract value, cycle length.
- Comp starting concept: 8–12% of first-month collected revenue; prepaid deals may use 3–6% of collected contract cash. Final plan requires CEO approval.

## Automation / Solutions

- Outcome: reliable systems launched on time.
- Track: 3–7 day deployment target, QA pass rate, defects, automation failures, resolution time.

## Client Success

- Outcome: retention, adoption, expansion, referrals.
- Track: gross retention, churn risk, review cadence, expansion, referrals, unresolved dependencies.

## Management Cadence

- Daily: blockers, hot opportunities, launch failures.
- Weekly: pipeline, delivery capacity, client health, cash/collections, KPI exceptions.
- Monthly: MRR, gross margin, churn, sales efficiency, channel performance, staffing capacity.
- Quarterly: offer/pricing review, vertical performance, partner/vendor review, strategic bets.
