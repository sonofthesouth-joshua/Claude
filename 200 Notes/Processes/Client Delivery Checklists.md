SHADOW MARKETING MEDIA
CLIENT DELIVERY CHECKLISTS
Implementation, QA, launch, optimization, and retention

## Pre-Launch Gate

- Agreement signed and payment confirmed
- Intake complete
- Access received
- Offer/ICP/qualification rules approved
- Pipeline and ownership approved
- AI knowledge and escalation approved
- Calendar and routing approved
- Tracking plan approved

## Build Checklist

- CRM stages and fields
- Forms and chat
- Phone/call tracking
- Missed-call recovery
- SMS/email follow-up
- Booking and reminders
- Audit routing
- Review workflow
- Analytics/conversion events
- Dashboards and notifications

## QA Checklist

- Test new lead from every source
- Test duplicate contact behavior
- Test missed call
- Test chat handoff
- Test booking and cancellation
- Test opt-out
- Test pipeline stage changes
- Test automation stop conditions
- Test owner notifications
- Test attribution fields
- Test report/dashboard visibility

## 30-Day Optimization

- Baseline vs current KPI review
- Broken workflow audit
- Lead quality review
- Speed-to-lead review
- Follow-up completion review
- Stale pipeline review
- Booking/show/close review
- Reputation review
- Client dependencies
- Next 30-day priorities

## Retention

Document wins and unresolved risks, maintain a single success plan, hold KPI reviews, identify expansion only after the core system is working, and request referrals/case studies only after demonstrated value.
