SHADOW MARKETING MEDIA
SALES SCRIPTS & OBJECTION HANDLING
Diagnostic selling for high-ticket revenue systems

## Opening

Start with the business, not the service list. Confirm why the prospect took the meeting, current growth target, and what they believe is blocking it.

## Core Diagnostic Questions

- Walk me through what happens from the second a lead calls, fills out a form, or sends a message.
- Who owns first response, and what is the real response time during business hours and after hours?
- What happens to missed calls?
- How many attempts happen before your team stops following up?
- How are open estimates, proposals, or no-shows worked?
- Can you see source → appointment → opportunity → closed revenue in one place?
- What happens to old leads and past customers?
- If I doubled your lead volume tomorrow, what part of your current process would break first?

## Problem Summary Framework

Reflect the prospect’s own numbers back: current demand → operational gap → financial exposure → business consequence → desired future state. Ask for confirmation before presenting scope.

## Objections


### “We need more leads.”

Separate demand from conversion. Ask what percentage of current leads receive immediate response and consistent follow-up before increasing spend.

### “We already have a CRM.”

A CRM is infrastructure. Ask whether it is actually enforcing ownership, response, follow-up, attribution, and reporting.

### “It’s too expensive.”

Return to the diagnosed economic gap, scope, implementation burden, and alternative cost. Do not fabricate ROI.

### “I need to think about it.”

Identify the unresolved issue: economics, trust, authority, timing, scope, or competing priority. Resolve the real objection rather than pressuring.

### “Send me information.”

Agree, then clarify what information would allow a decision and schedule a specific follow-up.

## Close

Confirm problem, desired result, scope, monthly investment, term, deployment dependencies, decision-maker approval, and next action. Closed-won requires signed agreement and confirmed payment.
