## CRM Pipeline

| Stage | Entry Criteria | Required Next Action | Exit Criteria | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New Lead | Contact captured | Respond and qualify | Contacted or disqualified | SDR / Automation |
| Attempting Contact | No live conversation yet | Multi-channel follow-up | Connected or nurture | SDR |
| Qualified | Fit, need and basic economics confirmed | Book discovery | Meeting scheduled | SDR / Closer |
| Discovery Scheduled | Meeting booked | Prepare audit/account brief | Meeting held/no-show | Closer |
| Discovery Complete | Problem and buying process mapped | Build recommendation/proposal | Proposal delivered | Closer |
| Proposal Sent | Scope and commercial terms delivered | Follow-up / decision call | Closed won/lost | Closer |
| Closed Won | Agreement + payment confirmed | Start onboarding | Client activated | Client Success |
| Closed Lost | Decision lost | Record reason; nurture if appropriate | Nurture/archived | Sales |
| Nurture | Not ready now | Timed value follow-up | Requalified | Automation / SDR |
