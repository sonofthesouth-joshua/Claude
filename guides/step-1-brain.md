# Step 1 — Install the Brain

**What you're building:** your company's knowledge — documents, offers, processes, clients, numbers — structured into this vault and distilled into nine core documents that describe *your* business, verified by you. Everything else in the kit runs on top of this.

**Time:** 2–4 hours of agent work, ~30 minutes of your attention (the interview + the refinement session). Large document sets can run across several sessions — the skill checkpoints and resumes.

## Before you start

1. **Gather the corpus.** One folder containing what your business runs on: offers and pricing docs, process docs, client material, team docs, reports. A messy folder is fine — that's normal. If it's in Google Drive, download the folders (File → Download) so they're local.
2. **Decide the exclusions.** Anything that must NOT enter the brain: payroll, HR records, personal legal, medical. You'll state these in the interview; the skill also runs its own sensitivity scan as a second net.
3. **Check the location.** The vault should live in a dot-free, non-cloud-synced path (e.g. `~/YourCompanyOS`, not inside `~/Documents` on macOS with iCloud on).

## Run it

Open your agent in the vault root and say:

> Read and follow `000 OS/skills/brain-install/SKILL.md`. My documents are at `<path to your folder>`.

The agent will inventory your files, interview you (~10 questions about the business), ask you to confirm what gets ingested, and then work through pull → convert → file → distill. Answer its questions; when it proposes the ingestion scope, actually read the list before saying yes.

## Your two important moments

- **The refinement session.** The agent produces `000 OS/install/refinement-agenda.md` — every conflict, gap, and unverified claim it found, plus "what changed recently that isn't in these documents?" Sit with it and rule on each item. This is what turns a document pile into *your* brain. Don't skip it; it's usually 20–30 minutes.
- **The quiz.** The agent asks itself 10 real questions about your business and answers only from the vault, citing sources. You judge the answers. Below 8/10 — refine and re-test.

## Done when

The nine core docs exist in `200 Notes/Company/`, the quiz passed, and asking the agent "what does my company do, who are my top clients, what's my tool stack?" gets a cited, correct answer.

**Try it for a week before Step 2:** ask the brain real questions during your workday. Every "not in my sources" it answers is telling you what to feed it next.
